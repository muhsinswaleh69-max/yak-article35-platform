# YAK Article 35 Platform – Access to Information
**Counties: Nairobi, Kajiado, Machakos**

Live Demo: https://yak-article35-platform.vercel.app

### Problem Solved
Citizens cannot find Bills, Policies, Budgets and participation dates. This platform centralizes Article 35 compliance.

### Features (TOR Aligned)
1. **Governance Repository** – Filter by County (Nairobi/Kajiado/Machakos), Category, Year, with Draft status highlighting for missing documents.
2. **Participation Hub** – Real-time countdown, Closing Soon RED badge (<3 days), locations.
3. **Memo Generator** – Guided form (What is good? What should change? What is missing?) – Exports Word and PDF compliant with County format.
4. **Response Tracker** – Kanban: Submitted -> Acknowledged -> Accepted/Rejected/Partially Adopted – ensures government feedback loop.

### Tech
Next.js 14, TypeScript, Tailwind, docx, jsPDF – Responsive, accessible, offline-ready.

### Compliance
Supports Article 35(a) & (b) – Right to information & timely publication. Audit log for Draft documents pending review.
